Calculation basis
Ideal power = steam flow (kg/s) × (h₁ − h₂s)
Shaft power = ideal power × turbine efficiency
Electrical output = shaft power × generator efficiency
Thermodynamic properties use the IAPWS-IF97 industrial formulation through an open-source steam-property library. Results are engineering estimates, not a guarantee of actual turbine performance. Confirm pressure basis, manufacturer efficiency, mechanical losses, and steam-flow measurement before using for design or contractual guarantees.
